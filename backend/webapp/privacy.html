DermAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-26

DermAI is an academic final-year project that provides a preliminary AI skin-screening aid and a teledermatology consultation service. This policy explains what we collect and why. DermAI is not a medical diagnosis; always consult a qualified dermatologist.

Information we collect

  • Account details — username, name, email, and (for patients) age and sex.
  • Medical history you choose to provide, used to inform the screening and the doctor.
  • Skin photos you upload for screening.
  • Consultation data — appointments, questionnaire answers, and doctors' notes.
  • Doctors additionally provide professional credentials and a licence image for verification.

How we use it

  • To run the AI screening and generate your report.
  • To connect you with a verified dermatologist you choose, and to share your report and medical history with that doctor for your consultation.
  • To verify doctor and management accounts.

Sharing

Your screening data and medical history are shared only with the dermatologist you book a consultation with, and with system administrators for verification and support. Photo analysis uses an on-server model; the conversational assistant sends a text summary (never your raw photo) to Google's Gemini API to generate questions and explanations.

Retention & your choices

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Authentication uses hashed passwords; we never store plaintext passwords.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the DermAI team (Hamdard University, Karachi).
